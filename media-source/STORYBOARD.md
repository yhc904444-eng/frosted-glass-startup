# Light becomes an interface / 14 seconds

A luminous bead connects material and interaction details in one continuous scene. English copy stays brief. The film is silent, covers the viewport, plays once, and holds its exact final image.

1. A moving highlight exposes three thin optical layers along the glass edge.
2. The camera approaches the send control. A two-axis press and rebound releases a halo and the light bead.
3. The bead arcs into the sidebar selection. A folder fans out miniature code and layout cards, then folds them away.
4. The light enters a floating search palette. Its selection slides between commands while the panels remain separated in depth.
5. Corners light as the panels seat. The bead enters the drawn Codex mark and triggers a ripple from that point.
6. Transient details settle away. The film ends on the complete interface.

`src/FilmDetails.tsx` defines the scene, `src/details.css` the added details, and `src/details-timeline.mjs` the shared time and camera model. `ripple.mjs` refracts the rendered pixels using that same camera. `render.mjs` selects the standard or wide composition in `src/index-startup-details.tsx`.

The earlier 32-second composition remains in `src/FilmEN.tsx` and `src/index-startup-hq.tsx` as a legacy source; the render commands use the new 14-second scene.
